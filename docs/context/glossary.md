# Glossary

Insurance domain terms you'll hit constantly. Kept here so the codebase stays legible.

- **AMS (Agency Management System)** — the agency's system of record (AMS360, Applied
  Epic). We sit on top of it; we never replace it.
- **Dec page (Declarations page)** — the policy summary: number, effective dates,
  limits, premium.
- **Endorsement** — a mid-term change to an in-force policy (add a vehicle/driver,
  change a limit, add an additional insured, address change). High-frequency, one of
  our two first-win targets.
- **ACORD form** — standardized industry application/data forms.
- **Loss run** — a carrier-provided history of a client's past claims.
- **CSR (Customer Service Representative)** — agency staff who service accounts.
- **Producer** — the agency salesperson / account owner.
- **Carrier** — the insurance company that underwrites the policy.
- **MGA / wholesaler** — intermediary you submit to for certain (often surplus-lines) risks.
- **Admitted vs. surplus lines** — admitted = state-regulated standard market; surplus
  lines = non-admitted specialty market (re-shopped every year).
- **Comparative rater** — third-party tool (EZLynx, QuoteRush, PL Rater) that quotes
  personal lines across carriers. We integrate; we don't rebuild.
- **COI (Certificate of Insurance)** — proof-of-coverage document clients request often.
- **Schedule of Insurance** — a client-facing summary of all their policies/coverages
  (Gracey-Backer's first quick-win target).
- **IVANS** — industry network for downloading policy/direct-bill data from carriers.
- **Bind** — to formally put coverage in force with the chosen carrier.
